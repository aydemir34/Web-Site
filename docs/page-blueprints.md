# Page Blueprints

## Standard 3-Page Structure

All basic demos must ship with exactly 3 public pages:
- Home
- Services
- Appointment & Contact

## Home Blueprint

Section order:
1. Header
2. Hero
3. Featured Services
4. Why Us
5. Gallery / Instagram Preview
6. Appointment CTA
7. Footer

Expected outcome:
- First screen clearly communicates service category and location.
- WhatsApp CTA appears early and clearly.
- Service previews push users toward deeper service exploration.

## Services Blueprint

Section order:
1. Header
2. Services Intro
3. Service Category Menu
4. Service Detail Blocks
5. FAQ
6. Appointment CTA
7. Footer

Expected outcome:
- Services page must feel structurally different from Home.
- Menu rhythm and detail hierarchy must guide scanning and selection.

## Appointment & Contact Blueprint

Section order:
1. Header
2. Contact Intro
3. Short About
4. Contact Info Panel
5. Social Links
6. Google Maps Embed
7. Opening Hours
8. FAQ
9. Appointment CTA
10. Footer

Expected outcome:
- Contact page is complete enough for immediate action.
- Visitor can find phone, WhatsApp, address, social accounts, and map without searching.
