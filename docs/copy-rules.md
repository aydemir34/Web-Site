# Copy Rules

## Turkish Language Integrity

Turkish-facing UI text must use correct Turkish characters:
- ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü

Broken transliteration in production-facing copy is not acceptable.

## English Usage Policy

Avoid unnecessary English words in public-facing Turkish text.

Allowed use:
- Brand language or service language where natural (for example: Hair & Makeup Studio, Makeup).

Disallowed use:
- Dense, unnecessary foreign wording inside Turkish sentences.

## Preferred Public Tone

Prefer natural Turkish phrasing such as:
- gelin
- kına
- nişan
- davet
- özel gün
- zarif
- hazırlık
- randevu bilgisi

Avoid overusing words like “bridal/editorial” in Turkish sentence flow unless required by brand context.

## Compliance & Claim Rules

- No medical/beauty result guarantees.
- No exaggerated claims like “best in Istanbul.”
- No before/after certainty language without verified assets and approval.

## WhatsApp Message Language

WhatsApp prefilled messages must be:
- Turkish
- natural and concise
- URL-encoded before use

Standard messages:
- general: `Merhaba, hizmetleriniz ve randevu bilgisi hakkında bilgi almak istiyorum.`
- service-specific: `Merhaba, {serviceName} hizmeti için randevu ve fiyat bilgisi almak istiyorum.`
